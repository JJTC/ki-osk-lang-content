Alhaji Oloye, Dauda Soroye Adegbenro Template:Wp/ibb/Audio (1909-1975) ama edi owo-ukara Naijiria, akamba owo nka Action Group (AG) ye Minista Isong ye Utom.
Enye ama enyene ukpono ọfọn eke mme owo mmọ ke Abeokuta, mọñ eke ẹma ẹnọ enye mme ikpọ itie itieubọñ nte Balogun ke Owu Egba ye Ekerin ke Egbaland .
Enye ke mana ke isua 1909 ke Ago-Owu, Abeokuta, Ogun State..
Dauda Soroye Adegbenro ama aka African School, Owowo n̄wed ufiop esie mbem iso ka Baptist Boys High School, Abeokuta ye Abeokuta Grammar School n̄wed ifiọk enyọn̄ esie.
Ötöñö utom mö ye Ndïto Ufök Utom Unam-Isöñ Ntre-Ikat Nigeria nte ewet-ñwed tönö ke afañ isua 1930 sïm 1937.
Ke ama ekebe oro, enye ama anam utom nte amama-ukpeme-ufök-ñkpö ke Ufök Utom Ndïto Africa Ayanga-Urua (UAC).
Adegbenro ikodukhe ke mbubehe ukara tutu esịm isua nsipe 1940.
Enye odụk otu mme Awolowo, Akintola ye mbon eken man ẹtọn̄ọ otu ukara Action Group ke ọfiọn̄ Akpatịp isua 1951.
Nka nnyịn ama owụh usụhọd nnyịn ke mbemiso elections 1951 ke Western Region House of Assembly, edi ama osio mme seat en̄wen ọnọ National Council of Nigeria and the Cameroons (NCNC) emi Adegoke Adelabu okodu usụn̄ ke Region oro.
Kpa ye ekemede nte mme utip emi ẹtode itọk-mbre-ukara ẹkedọñọ unwọñọ ẹnọ NCNC, nñwọñọ-ñwed mme owo ẹbañade ñka Action Group (AG) emi ẹkewọrọde ke itọk-mbre-ukara oro ẹkenọ mmọ uwak owo.
Adegbenro, kpa ntre, ama enyene edikan ke ndo ndo oro ke edimek efen nte edide owo emi odude ke inua owo ke mbume mbre ukara afan̄ Egba.
Ndien ikebighike, Nka Ukara Afan̄ oro ama emek enye nte kiet ke otu mme andida ke ibuot mmo ke Ufọk Ibet Ofuri Idut ke Lagos.
Enye ama afiak aka Nsukka Utom Ibet (Western Region House of Assembly) ke isua 1954 man akana nte Sēketeri (Parliamentary Secretary) ọnọ Akwa Ọbọn̄ Ibet (Minister of Justice), ekem nte Akwa Ọbọn̄ Isọn̄ ye Utom (Minister of Land and Labour), ye Akwa Ọbọn̄ Ukara M̀mītịm Isọn̄ (Minister of Local Government).
Enye akedi okwukwo, anam-akpanikọ, onyung awo eke ọfiọkọdọhọ ikọ andise mban̄a utom.
Enye ama enyene ata ọkpọsọn̄ ediana kiet ye mme ada-usūn̄ n̄ka-ukara ye ukara mọ, akpan-akpan Chief Awolowo emi ekedi Premier Region ini oro.
Enye ama osụk aka iso ado anenam utom ye Akwa Owo (Chief) kpa ye oro Akwa Owo oro ama akakabade akama ikpọ utom ke idụt nte Ada-usụn̄ Mbon Ubak-Ufọk Ukara ke Ufọk Ibet Ofuri Idụt.

Gboyega AribisoganTemplate:Wp/ibb/Audio edi owo politiki ke Nigeria emi ama anam utom nte owo nka iko (speaker) ke Ufok Ukpebe Ikop Ekiti State ke Obio Ikpa (November) 2022.
Enye edi owo nka-idem iba emi ada nno itie Ikole I constituency.
Aribisogan eke oto Ijesha-Isu ke Ikole Local Government Area, ke North senatorial district ke Ekiti State, ẹma ẹmek enye nte Speaker ufọk ibet ke usen 15 ọfiọn̄ November 2022 ke mkpa Funminiyi Afuye.
Ẹma ẹsio enye ke itie utom ke usen 21 ọfiọn̄ November 2022, usen itiọkịet ke ama akabọ itie oro Gboyega Aribisogan edi andiñwọñọ idem unyam-urua ukara ke Nigeria, onyung edi andisine ke Ufọk Ibet Ido Ẹkiti (Ekiti State House of Assembly) andida ke afan̄ Ikole Constituency I.
Ke ọyọhọ isua unam utom enye ọyọhọ iba, enye akanam utom nte Akpan Owo Unyam-Urua Ukara (Leader of Government Business) ke Ufọk Ibet emi mbemiso ẹmek enye nte Akwa Andida Iso Ufọk Ibet (Speaker) ke ọfiọn̄ Ọyọhọ Duopeba ke isua 2022.
Idem n̄kpọ n̄kpa Speaker Funminiyi Afuye keenyọn̄ October 2022, Ekiti State House of Assembly ama ọtọn̄ọ utom ndiwụt obufa Speaker.
Ke usen 15 November 2022, a Aribisogan ama akabade Speaker ke emi enye okoyom n̄kpọ 15 votes ndikan Olubunmi Adelugba, emi okosongode 10 votes.
Clerk of the House, Tola Esan ama anam enye odụk uduak enye nte Speaker ke ini n̄kpọ emi okokụre.
Enye ama owụt nko uduak esie ndinam ufan ye edidianakiet odo mme mbon ufọk ibet nnyụn̄ n̄kụt nte ke emen utom ibet eke Ufọk Ibet odo asan̄a ọfọn.
Ke usen 21 November 2022, usen ita usen ita ita (eke isen) ke emana esie, mme owo nka utom mme usen etop ke Ekiti State ikebode Aribisogan nte nte-nneme ke mbono nka utom.
Ke otode ndọhọ esie, ẹma ẹmek Olubunmi Adelugba nte Obio Speaker mme Speaker obufa, ndien enye akabade edi akpa an̄wan emi odude ke utom oro ke mbuk obio state oro.
