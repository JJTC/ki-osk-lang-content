# phone: long vowels often written short (i for ई), the retroflex
# consonants as the dental ones (t for ट), a word's last a usually long
# (kya: क्या), and the vowel between two consonants left out where it is
# not spoken (samjha: समझा). The lexicon chooses among the readings; the
# costs (osk_data::translit) say how usual each is.
# English words written in Hindi's script are typed as English spells
# them (school: स्कूल, doctor: डॉक्टर): c, ck, sch, o as ॉ, u as अ or यू, a
# silent last e, er, or, le, y and tion have English ways too, at a cost.
# Its costs are fitted to how people romanized the Dakshina dataset's
# training words (CC BY-SA 4.0; make fit-translit, NOTICES.md), each
# held to the one first written here.
