# Sinhala from Latin letters, as Sinhala is typed in Latin ("Singlish").
# Sinhala writes the vowel it speaks between consonants, so two consonants
# typed together are joined by the al-lakuna, as is a word's last
# consonant (wadan: වදන්); r and y after one join as its rakaransaya and
# yansaya, which Sinhala text writes with a zero-width joiner after the
# al-lakuna: the second way of `r` and `y` below begins with one (U+200D),
# invisible here, and reads only after a consonant. v and w are both ව, t
# and d both the dental and the retroflex letter, e mostly the æ of ඇ, and
# the lexicon chooses.
# English words, which the language writes in its script too, are typed
# as English spells them: c, ck, sch, o, er, y and tion have English
# ways too, at a cost.
# Its costs are fitted to how people romanized the Dakshina dataset's
# training words (CC BY-SA 4.0; make fit-translit, NOTICES.md), each
# held to the one first written here.
