# Meitei (Meetei Mayek) from Latin letters, as Meitei is typed in Latin.
# A consonant carries the vowel a; a typed a may be that or ꯥ, the long
# one (mahak: ꯃꯍꯥꯛ), and the lexicon chooses. A syllable's last k, l, m,
# p, n, t or ng, before another consonant or at a word's end, is its
# lonsum letter (ꯛ ꯜ ꯝ ꯞ ꯟ ꯠ ꯡ: masing: ꯃꯁꯤꯡ, henna: ꯍꯦꯟꯅ); other
# consonants typed together join with the apun iyek, as loanwords do
# (Indra: ꯏꯟꯗ꯭ꯔ). A word's first i and u are the letters ꯏ and ꯎ, its
# first e may be ꯏ too, as names are spelled (Ema: ꯏꯃꯥ), ai and oi end
# in ꯏ (or ꯢ), ao is ꯥꯎ (chaaoba: ꯆꯥꯎꯕ), ei and ou are ꯩ and ꯧ; a
# syllable's last ng may be ꯪ, and before a vowel too ꯡ (laangon:
# ꯂꯥꯡꯑꯣꯟ).
