# Nepali (Devanagari) from Latin letters, as Nepali is typed in Latin:
# Hindi's ways, with Nepali's own: cha for छ as often as for च (ramro
# cha: राम्रो छ), few nuktas (z, f and q are ज, फ and क first), a word's
# last consonant often with its virama (garchhan: गर्छन्), n before a
# consonant as often ँ as ं (aansu: आँसु, bataunchha: बताउँछ), au as often
# आउ as औ, ng and nk as ङ (darjeeling: दार्जिलिङ, ganga: गङ्गा) and nch
# and nj as ञ (panchayat: पञ्चायत), b sometimes for व. Long vowels are
# often written short, the retroflex consonants as the dental ones, and
# the lexicon chooses. English words written in Nepali's script are typed
# as English spells them, as Hindi's table reads them, and a and i have
# their English ways too (plate: प्लेट, side: साइड).
