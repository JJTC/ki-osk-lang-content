# Konkani (Devanagari) from Latin letters, as Konkani is typed in Latin:
# Marathi's ways (long vowels often written short, t and d for ट and ड as
# well, l for ळ as well, the vowel between two consonants left out where
# it is not spoken), with Konkani's own: z for ज, which Konkani says z
# (zata: जाता), x for श as Konkani's Roman spelling writes it (xala:
# शाळा), ण as often as न, a word's last vowel nasal (ं) where a last m
# or n is typed for it (hem: हें, tanne: ताणें, lagim: लागीं), and as
# Konkani's typists also write: dh for ड (vadhli: वाडली), lh for ळ
# (palhe: पळे), ie for ई and ien for ईं (ghalien: घालीं), ey for a last ए.
# English words written in Konkani's script are typed as English spells
# them (school: स्कूल): c, ck, sch, o as ॉ, u as अ or यू, a silent last e,
# er, or, le, y and tion have English ways too, at a cost.
