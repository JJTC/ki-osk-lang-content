# Saraiki (Shahmukhi, Perso-Arabic script) from Latin letters, as
# Saraiki is typed in Latin. As Western Punjabi's table: short vowels
# mostly unwritten inside a word, a word's first vowel on an alif, its
# last e ے, several letters for one sound (s: س ص ث, z: ز ذ ض ظ), h after
# a consonant its aspirate, ݨ for the retroflex n; with Saraiki's own
# implosives ٻ ڄ ݙ ڳ, which Latin has no letters for: typed as the plain
# letters, or doubled (gaya: ڳیا, ditta: ݙتا, bahon: ٻہوں). The lexicon
# chooses.
# As Urdu's table does, it writes a consonant typed twice once (taraqqi:
# ترقی), a vowel as the ع it is said with (istemaal: استعمال), a word's
# last o as its nasal وں (dono: دونوں) and its last ay as ے.
