# Urdu (Perso-Arabic script) from Latin letters, as Urdu is typed in
# Latin. The script leaves short vowels unwritten inside a word: a, i and
# u there are mostly nothing, a word's first vowel rides an alif, its last
# a is ا or ہ and its last e ے; several letters share a sound (s: س ص ث, z:
# ز ذ ض ظ, t: ت ط), and h after a consonant is the ھ of its aspirate (bh:
# بھ). The lexicon chooses among them.
# English words, which the language writes in its script too, are typed
# as English spells them: c, ck, sch, o, er, y and tion have English
# ways too, at a cost.
# Its costs are fitted to how people romanized the Dakshina dataset's
# training words (CC BY-SA 4.0; make fit-translit, NOTICES.md), each
# held to the one first written here.
