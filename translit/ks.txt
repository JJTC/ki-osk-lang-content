# Kashmiri (Perso-Arabic script) from Latin letters, as Kashmiri is typed
# in Latin. Kashmiri's spelling marks its vowels, more or less fully as
# the writer chooses: a short vowel is a mark over or under its consonant
# (zabar َ, zer ِ, pesh ُ, and Kashmiri's own ٔ and ٕ for its central
# vowel, ٚ for e and o) or nothing, a long one a letter (ا ی و ے ۄ ٲ),
# and a word's last vowel often ہ with its mark (teh: تہٕ, yih: یہِ). ts
# is ژ, kh, th, ph and chh are the aspirates with ھ, and n before a
# consonant may carry ٛ (manz: مَنٛز). The lexicon chooses among them.
