# unwritten inside a word, a word's first vowel on an alif, its last e ے,
# several letters for one sound (s: س ص ث, z: ز ذ ض ظ), h after a consonant
# its aspirate; with Punjabi's own: ݨ for its retroflex n, a last u nasal
