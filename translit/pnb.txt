# Western Punjabi (Shahmukhi, Perso-Arabic script) from Latin letters, as
# Punjabi is typed in Latin. As Urdu's table: short vowels mostly
# unwritten inside a word, a word's first vowel on an alif, its last e ے,
# several letters for one sound (s: س ص ث, z: ز ذ ض ظ), h after a consonant
# its aspirate; with Punjabi's own: ݨ for its retroflex n, a last u nasal
# (nu: نوں), ae for اے. The lexicon chooses.
# As Urdu's table does, it writes a consonant typed twice once (taraqqi:
# ترقی), a vowel as the ع it is said with (istemaal: استعمال), a word's
# last o as its nasal وں (dono: دونوں) and its last ay as ے.
