# Brahui (Perso-Arabic script) from Latin letters, as Brahui is typed in
# Latin: Urdu's ways, whose script and spelling Brahui writes with (short
# vowels mostly unwritten inside a word, a word's first vowel on an alif,
# h after a consonant its aspirate), with Brahui's own: ۺ, its lateral
# fricative, typed lh, a first i on ئ (is: ئس), and the words ءِ and ءُ
# typed e and u. The lexicon chooses.
# As Urdu's table does, it writes a consonant typed twice once (taraqqi:
# ترقی), a vowel as the ع it is said with (istemaal: استعمال), a word's
# last o as its nasal وں (dono: دونوں) and its last ay as ے.
