# Khowar (Perso-Arabic script) from Latin letters, as Khowar is typed in
# Latin: Urdu's ways and their costs, fitted to how people romanized
# Urdu's words (the Dakshina dataset's; NOTICES.md), whose script and
# spelling Khowar writes with (short vowels mostly unwritten inside a
# word, a word's first vowel on an alif, h after a consonant its
# aspirate), with Khowar's own letters: its retroflex ݯ ݮ ݰ ݱ beside ch,
# j, sh and zh's چ ج ش ژ, at a cost (chitrar: ݯھترار), and څ and ځ for ts
# and dz. The lexicon chooses.
# English words, which the language writes in its script too, are typed
# as English spells them: c, ck, sch, o, er, y and tion have English
# ways too, at a cost.
