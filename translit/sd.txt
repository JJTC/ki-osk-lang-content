# Sindhi (Perso-Arabic script) from Latin letters, as Sindhi is typed in
# Latin. The script leaves short vowels unwritten: a, i and u inside a
# word are mostly nothing, and at its end often too (para: پر, hiku: هڪ),
# its first vowel rides an alif, its last o is و, and its last a ا. Sindhi has letters of its own for its sounds:
# the implosives ٻ ڄ ڏ ڳ (typed b, j, d, g as the others are), the
# retroflexes ٽ ڊ ڙ ڻ, the aspirates ڀ ٿ ٺ ڦ ڇ ڌ ڍ ک, k as ڪ, and two
# words written as signs: ۾ (men, in) and ۽ (ain, and). The lexicon
# chooses among them.
