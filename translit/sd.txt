# Sindhi (Perso-Arabic script) from Latin letters, as Sindhi is typed in
# Latin. The script leaves short vowels unwritten: a, i and u inside a
# word are mostly nothing, and at its end often too (para: پر, hiku: هڪ),
# its first vowel rides an alif, its last o is و, and its last a ا. Sindhi has letters of its own for its sounds:
# the implosives ٻ ڄ ڏ ڳ (typed b, j, d, g as the others are), the
# retroflexes ٽ ڊ ڙ ڻ, the aspirates ڀ ٿ ٺ ڦ ڇ ڌ ڍ ک, k as ڪ, and two
# words written as signs: ۾ (men, in) and ۽ (ain, and). The lexicon
# chooses among them. Its h is ه: the texts write ھ for it too, and the
# lexicon reads the two as one letter (osk_data::words, generation 3).
# The nasal its pronouns write before their h is typed after it (jahin,
# jenh: جنهن; pahinje: پنهنجي), the hamza's seat is typed as a vowel
# (huwa: هئا, ie: ئي), a word's last i may take ءَ and its last aye ءِ
# (zindagi: زندگيءَ, laye: لاءِ), and b alone is به, as chat writes it.
# English words, which the language writes in its script too, are typed
# as English spells them: c, ck, sch, o, er, y and tion have English
# ways too, at a cost.
# Its costs are fitted to how people romanized the Dakshina dataset's
# training words (CC BY-SA 4.0; make fit-translit, NOTICES.md), each
# held to the one first written here.
