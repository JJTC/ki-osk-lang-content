# Santali (Ol Chiki) from Latin letters, as Santali is typed in Latin. A
# letter for each sound, but several share a Latin letter (o: ᱚ and ᱳ,
# a: ᱟ and ᱟᱹ, t: ᱛ and ᱴ, d: ᱫ and ᱰ, r: ᱨ and ᱲ, w: ᱣ and ᱶ), and h after a
# consonant is its aspirate (kh: ᱠᱷ). A word's last k, c, t and p are
# checked, and written ᱜ ᱡ ᱫ ᱵ (menak: ᱢᱮᱱᱟᱜ, mit: ᱢᱤᱫ), with ᱼ before a
# vowel that follows (menaka: ᱢᱮᱱᱟᱜᱼᱟ); an apostrophe marks one as
# Santali's Latin spelling does (mit'). A last g, j, d or b that is no
# checked consonant takes the ahad (ᱜᱽ ᱡᱽ ᱫᱽ ᱵᱽ). n after a vowel at a
# word's end may be its nasalization ᱸ (tahen: ᱛᱟᱦᱮᱸ). T D N R are the
# retroflex letters, for those who type them so. The lexicon chooses
# among them.
