# Adilabad Gondi (Telugu script) from Latin letters, as Gondi is typed in
# Latin: Telugu's ways, whose script the Gondi of Telangana is written
# in, and its costs, fitted to how people romanized Telugu's words (the
# Dakshina dataset's; NOTICES.md). Two consonants typed together are
# joined by the virama; a word's last m (and n) is the nasal sign, any
# other last consonant takes the virama, as Gondi's many words that end
# in one do (koytur: కోయ్తూర్, gond: గోండ్).
# English words, which the language writes in its script too, are typed
# as English spells them: c, ck, sch, o, er, y and tion have English
# ways too, at a cost.
