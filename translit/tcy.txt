# Tulu (Kannada script) from Latin letters, as Tulu is typed in Latin:
# Kannada's ways, whose script Tulu is written in. Tulu writes every vowel
# it speaks, so two consonants typed together are joined by the virama;
# a word's last m is the nasal sign, and any other last consonant takes
# the virama, as Tulu's many words that end in one do (malter: ಮಲ್ತೆರ್,
# mast: ಮಸ್ತ್).
# English words, which the language writes in its script too, are typed
# as English spells them: c, ck, sch, o, er, y and tion have English
# ways too, at a cost.
