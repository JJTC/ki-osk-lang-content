# Odia (Odia script) from Latin letters, as Odia is typed in Latin. Odia
# speaks the vowel a consonant carries, typed a or o (bhala: ଭଲ), a for
# ା as well; t and d are ତ and ଦ or ଟ and ଡ, d also ଡ଼ (odia: ଓଡ଼ିଆ), y is
# ୟ or ଯ, l ଲ or ଳ, and the lexicon chooses. As Odia writes them: nk is
# ଙ୍କ (pilanku: ପିଲାଙ୍କୁ), nch ଞ୍ଚ, rt and rdh ର୍ତ୍ତ and ର୍ଦ୍ଧ (kartabya:
# କର୍ତ୍ତବ୍ୟ), ai mostly ାଇ and au often ାଉ, ru ୃ (drusya: ଦୃଶ୍ୟ), khy and
# gy କ୍ଷ and ଜ୍ଞ, a last ay or ey େ (marilay: ମାରିଲେ), and n before a
# consonant ଂ or ଁ (kahinki: କାହିଁକି).
