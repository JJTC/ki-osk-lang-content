# Tamil (Tamil script) from Latin letters, as Tamil is typed in Latin.
# Tamil writes every vowel it speaks, so two consonants typed together
# are joined by the puḷḷi, as is a word's last consonant (avan: அவன்).
# One letter stands for voiced and voiceless alike (k and g: க), n is ந
# at a word's start and ன after, and l, n, r and t each have several
# letters the lexicon chooses among (ல ள ழ, ன ந ண, ர ற, ட த). Its
# doubled consonants are as often typed single (thaakuthal: தாக்குதல்,
# mathipeedu: மதிப்பீடு), its ோ as oa or oe (poattigal: போட்டிகள்).
# English words, which the language writes in its script too, are typed
# as English spells them: c, ck, sch, o, er, y and tion have English
# ways too, at a cost.
# Its costs are fitted to how people romanized the Dakshina dataset's
# training words (CC BY-SA 4.0; make fit-translit, NOTICES.md), each
# held to the one first written here.
