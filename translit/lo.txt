# Lao from Latin letters, as Lao is typed in Latin: kh th ph for the
# aspirates (ຂ ຄ, ຖ ທ, ຜ ພ), ny for ຍ, x or s for ຊ, ch or j for ຈ, a
# syllable's last k t p for ກ ດ ບ; a long vowel typed short or doubled
# (aa, ee, oo), ae for ແ, eu for ຶ ື, oe or er for ເ◌ີ, o for ໂ, ◌ົ and
# the open o ◌ອ ◌ໍ. A vowel's first part is written before its consonant
# (◌: ke is ເກ, kao ເກົາ), and a vowel no consonant carries is carried
# by ອ (ai: ອ້າຍ). The high consonants Lao writes before a low one (ໜ ໝ
# ຫລ ຫງ ຫຍ ຫວ) and the tone marks are nothing typed: the reading takes
# them where the lexicon has a word so written (kao: ເກົ້າ). Lao writes
# no space between its words. The lexicon chooses.
