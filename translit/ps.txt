# Pashto (Perso-Arabic script) from Latin letters, as Pashto is typed in
# Latin. Short vowels mostly go unwritten inside a word; a word's last a
# is ه (khabara: خبره), its last e ې, its last ay ی or ۍ. Pashto has
# letters of its own for its sounds (ټ ډ ړ ڼ for the retroflexes, ښ ږ, ځ
# څ, ګ), typed as their nearest Latin ones. The lexicon chooses.
