# Maithili (Devanagari) from Latin letters, as Maithili is typed in Latin:
# Hindi's ways, which Maithili's writers follow (long vowels often written
# short, the retroflex consonants as the dental ones, a word's last a
# usually long, the vowel between two consonants left out where it is not
# spoken), and English words as English spells them. Maithili's own: ँ
# where Hindi writes ं, before a consonant and at a word's end (paanch:
# पाँच, hamarasan: हमरासँ, also typed sean), ae as two vowels (sakae:
# सकए), a last ai often ाइ (jaai: जाइ), d and dh for ड़ (khidhaki:
# खिड़की), and h at a word's end the visarga (prayah: प्रायः). The lexicon
# chooses.
